Child Safety Standards and Protection Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

Around is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users and to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

Around has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse, exploitation, grooming, or any other behavior that places children at risk.

Zero-Tolerance Policy

Around strictly prohibits the creation, sharing, requesting, promotion, facilitation, or distribution of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any behavior that sexually exploits or endangers children.

These standards apply to all users, content, communications, temporary statuses, rooms, profiles, reactions, and other functionality available through Around.

2. Scope of These Standards

These Child Safety Standards apply to everyone using Around, regardless of age, location, or whether the interaction occurs through a temporary status, nearby discovery, room, or other feature.

Around is designed for local, temporary social communication. It is not intended to facilitate sexual activity, exploitation, or inappropriate interactions involving minors.

3. Prohibited Child-Safety Conduct

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Around:

  • Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
  • Creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, storing, or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Grooming or attempting to establish contact with a child for sexual exploitation.
  • Sexual exploitation or sexualization of minors.
  • Sexual solicitation involving minors.
  • Threatening, coercing, blackmailing, or otherwise exploiting a child.
  • Facilitating trafficking or commercial sexual exploitation involving children.
  • Encouraging another person to engage in child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Using Around to arrange or facilitate abusive interactions involving children.
  • Attempting to circumvent safety controls or moderation mechanisms for the purpose of exploiting a child.
Important:

Any suspected CSAM or CSAE activity may result in immediate removal of content, blocking or suspension of the relevant account or device, preservation of relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting to appropriate authorities when required by applicable law.

4. Other Safety Violations

Around also does not permit behavior that creates an unsafe environment for users, including:

  • Harassment or targeted abuse.
  • Threats or intimidation.
  • Bullying or repeated unwanted contact.
  • Sexual harassment.
  • Sharing another person's private information without appropriate permission.
  • Impersonation intended to deceive, harm, or exploit another person.
  • Content intended to facilitate abuse or exploitation.

5. Reporting Safety Concerns

Users can report safety concerns directly through the Around application.

The reporting functionality is intended to allow users to report content or behavior that may violate these standards.

Reports may include concerns relating to:

  • Child safety
  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation
  • Suspected CSAM
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threatening behavior
  • Inappropriate content
  • Other safety concerns
If you see something unsafe:

Use the reporting function inside Around as soon as possible. You may also contact the Child Safety Point of Contact listed below.

6. Review and Enforcement

Around takes safety reports seriously and may investigate reports and take appropriate action consistent with these standards and applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting violating content.
  • Blocking or restricting users.
  • Suspending or terminating access to Around.
  • Preventing continued abuse of Around's features.
  • Preserving relevant information where appropriate and legally permitted.
  • Reporting suspected or confirmed illegal activity to appropriate authorities where required by law.

Around may take immediate action when necessary to protect users or address serious safety risks.

7. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Around strictly prohibits CSAM.

Users must never create, request, upload, share, distribute, store, or otherwise facilitate CSAM through Around.

If Around obtains actual knowledge of CSAM or other child sexual exploitation content, appropriate action will be taken in accordance with these standards, applicable law, and relevant regulatory requirements.

This may include removal or restriction of the content, restriction of the responsible user, preservation of relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting to the appropriate authorities or organizations where required.

9. Cooperation With Authorities

Around will cooperate with Google Play and relevant authorities regarding child-safety matters as required by applicable laws and regulations.

Where legally required, Around will follow applicable procedures for reporting confirmed CSAM and other serious child-safety violations to the appropriate authority or designated reporting organization.

Around may also respond to lawful requests from law-enforcement or regulatory authorities concerning child-safety investigations.

10. Safety Guidance for Users

Users should take reasonable precautions when interacting with people they do not know.

  • Do not share passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal information with other users.
  • Do not share private information about another person without appropriate permission.
  • Block or report users who make you feel unsafe.
  • Do not respond to requests for inappropriate or exploitative content.
  • If an interaction appears unsafe, stop communicating and use the reporting functionality.
  • When meeting someone known only through an online service, use appropriate real-world safety precautions and involve a trusted adult where appropriate.
