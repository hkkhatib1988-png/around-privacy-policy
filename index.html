Around

Privacy Policy for Around — a proximity-based social application for people nearby.

Last Updated: October 7, 2026
Your privacy matters.

Around is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. The application is built around local, proximity-based communication and does not require users to create an account or provide their real name, email address, or phone number for its core functionality.

1. Information We Collect

Around is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Depending on the features you use, the application may process the following information:

Anonymous Profile Information

You may create a temporary nickname or anonymous identity to use within the application.

This nickname is intended to identify you within nearby interactions and does not need to contain your real name or other personal information.

Statuses and Content

You may voluntarily create information such as:

  • Temporary statuses
  • Short messages or requests
  • Reactions
  • Replies
  • Nearby room information
  • Other content that you choose to share with nearby users

This information is intended to be exchanged with compatible nearby devices using supported proximity technologies.

Device and Connectivity Information

The application may use Android device connectivity capabilities required to discover and communicate with nearby devices.

Depending on your Android version and enabled features, this may include Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), Nearby Devices permissions, NFC, Wi-Fi-related peer-to-peer capabilities, or similar Android connectivity services.

The application does not use these capabilities to determine or continuously track your precise physical location.

2. Location Information

Around does not require GPS location tracking for its core functionality.

The application does not collect, store, or transmit your precise GPS coordinates to a remote server.

Android may associate certain Bluetooth or nearby-device permissions with location-related permissions depending on the Android version and device configuration. Such Android platform requirements do not mean that Around is collecting or storing your GPS location.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information processed by Around is used only to provide the application's functionality, including:

  • Displaying nearby statuses
  • Allowing users to share temporary statuses
  • Enabling nearby interactions
  • Managing temporary rooms
  • Processing reactions and replies
  • Applying expiration times to temporary content
  • Providing privacy and blocking controls
  • Improving application reliability and performance

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Local and Nearby Communication

Around is designed around proximity-based communication.

Where supported, information may be exchanged directly between nearby devices using technologies such as Bluetooth Low Energy and other Android peer-to-peer communication mechanisms.

Content shared through nearby communication may be visible to compatible Around users within the applicable communication range.

You should therefore avoid publishing sensitive, confidential, financial, authentication, or other private information in a public nearby status.

5. Data Storage

Around is designed to store application data locally on your device whenever practical.

Temporary statuses and other application information may be stored locally so that the application can provide its functionality.

The application does not require a permanent cloud account for its core functionality.

If a future feature introduces remote or cloud functionality, this Privacy Policy will be updated before that functionality is introduced.

6. Data Retention

Around is designed around temporary interactions.

Statuses, rooms, requests, and other temporary content may automatically expire according to the duration selected by the user or the application's applicable rules.

You may also delete locally stored application data through the application or through Android's application settings, where supported.

Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data according to Android's standard behavior.

7. Third-Party Services

The core functionality of Around does not require advertising networks, social-media accounts, or third-party cloud services.

The application may use services provided by Google and the Android operating system that are necessary for application distribution, billing, security, or platform functionality.

Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

8. Google Play Billing

If Around offers paid features or a one-time purchase through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play.

Around does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Google may process purchase information necessary to complete and manage transactions.

You can review Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy

9. Advertising

Around does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising purposes.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of the application, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising technologies and data practices.

10. Analytics and Diagnostics

The application may use limited technical information for application reliability, troubleshooting, or diagnostics where necessary.

Such information may include technical information such as application version, operating system version, device-related technical information, and crash or error information.

We do not use diagnostic information to intentionally identify you personally.

If third-party analytics or crash-reporting services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

11. User-Controlled Privacy Features

Around may provide features such as:

  • Blocking nearby users
  • Muting users
  • Removing your own statuses
  • Choosing how long a status remains visible
  • Controlling whether you participate in nearby discovery
  • Leaving temporary rooms
  • Clearing locally stored application data

The availability of specific controls may depend on the version of the application and Android operating system.

12. Children's Privacy

Around is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

The application does not require users to provide their real name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal information to use its core functionality.

Parents or guardians should encourage children to avoid sharing sensitive personal information through public nearby statuses or messages.

13. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application.

However, no electronic communication or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because Around enables nearby communication, users should carefully consider the information they choose to share with people within communication range.

14. Data Sharing

Around does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Information may be shared with nearby users when you intentionally use features that make your status, request, room, reaction, or other content available to them.

Information may also be processed by Google or other service providers when required to provide platform, billing, security, or technical services.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's functionality, technologies, or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

We encourage users to periodically review this page.
